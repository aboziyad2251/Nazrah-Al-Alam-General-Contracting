# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary**: B2B decision-makers — project owners, real estate developers, heavy and light industry operators, facility managers, and business owners across Saudi Arabia.

**Context**: Visitors arrive with a specific need (construction project, equipment rental, security installation, manpower supply). They are evaluating credibility and capability before making contact. They may be comparing multiple firms. The site must win trust in under 60 seconds.

**Language**: Bilingual audience — Arabic-first for Saudi nationals, English for expat professionals and international firms operating in the Kingdom.

## Product Purpose

Nazrah Al Alam General Contracting (Est. 1999, Jeddah) is a full-service general contracting firm offering 10 disciplines: civil & structural, mechanical, electrical, infrastructure, maintenance, fit-out, equipment rental, CCTV & security, networking, and manpower supply.

**What success looks like**: A decision-maker lands on the site, sees proof of capability (25+ years, modern fleet, real project photos), finds their service, and initiates contact — via WhatsApp inquiry or quote form submission.

## Brand Personality

**Powerful. Trustworthy. Modern.**

- Powerful: heavy-industry weight — bold typography, dark navy, large-scale imagery that signals serious capability.
- Trustworthy: no hype, no empty claims — every statement backed by numbers, credentials, or real photos. Saudi roots, decades of delivery.
- Modern: not a legacy firm stuck in the past — clean layout, bilingual UX, digital-first engagement (WhatsApp CTA, online quote).

Emotional goal: a visitor should feel they are dealing with the most capable and reliable contractor in the room.

## Anti-references

- **Cheap/cluttered local directories**: no wall-of-text service lists, no low-res stock photos, no rainbow color schemes.
- **Cold corporate banks**: no sterile white-on-grey, no impersonal language, no buried contact info.
- **Flashy nightclub/entertainment sites**: no gratuitous animation, no dark-mode neon, no design that prioritizes spectacle over credibility.

## References

Sites admired for professionalism and service clarity in the Saudi/Gulf market:

- manar.com.sa — clean authority positioning for a Saudi contracting firm
- aflak.com.sa — focused service presentation, CCTV/access control vertical

What to borrow: clear service hierarchy, confident use of whitespace, professional photography, easy-to-find contact paths.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through proof** — every claim is anchored by evidence: years of operation, fleet count, project photos, client names. Never decorate without substance.
2. **Authority without arrogance** — visual weight (navy, bold type, large imagery) conveys strength; gold warmth and direct Arabic/English copy keep it human and approachable.
3. **Clarity drives conversion** — each page section has one job. WhatsApp and quote form are always one tap away. No dead ends.
4. **Bilingual as a feature, not a fallback** — Arabic (RTL, Cairo/IBM Plex) and English receive equal typographic care. Language switching never feels like an afterthought.
5. **Show scale** — large equipment photos, service images, and real project shots do the selling. The fleet and team size are a competitive advantage; make them visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Both client-facing site and admin panel must be keyboard-navigable and screen-reader friendly. Bilingual (AR/EN) RTL/LTR switching must not break focus order or layout. Color contrast ratios must pass AA on both navy and white backgrounds.
